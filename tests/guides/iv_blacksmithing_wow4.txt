Leveling Blacksmithing from 1 to 75

Start with  Rough Sharpening Stones, then switch to  Rough Grinding Stones at 25. Keep the grinding stones until you reach 80. You'll need two for each pair of  Runed Copper Gauntlets and ten more if you make  Silver Rods at 75. Craft the gauntlets at an anvil with your  Blacksmith Hammer.

At 50, start making  Coarse Grinding Stones. You'll need 36 for  Patterned Bronze Bracers later. Once you've made all 36, check your skill and make just enough gauntlets to reach 75.

Leveling Blacksmithing from 75 to 150

Learn Journeyman Blacksmithing before crafting past 75. If you have ten  Rough Grinding Stones left, compare the price of one  Silver Bar with ten  Copper Bars.
